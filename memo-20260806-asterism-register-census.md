## E. The open fork, framed for the operator's ruling

Whether a barred register **CONSTRAINS** a future mint outright, or **RECORDS** evidence the minter
weighs against the case at hand. Evidence found on both sides:

- *Toward CONSTRAINS*: JJSAV treats the bar as class-binding ("the bar is what binds… a minter who reads
  only the word list will re-derive the class and walk into it again"); JJSAO says "rejects both registers
  estate-wide"; both consumers treat the ruling as settled law, not a factor.
- *Toward RECORDS*: each bar's reason is situational — audible-register failure against a *live* colonization,
  a load-bearing word *currently* spent — so an unconditional bar would outlive its reason; the soak memo
  itself preserves the Roman runner-up's argument for revival; MCM's reservation doctrine holds "a reserved
  word is not unmintable in itself", and the soil-family censuses grow by discovery rather than settling in advance.
- *A middle reading the sources support*: a bar CONSTRAINS while its recorded reason stands, and only a
  deliberate ruling — never a minter's own weighing mid-election — retires the bar with its reason.
