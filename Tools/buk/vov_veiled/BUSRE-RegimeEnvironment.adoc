{bure_regime}

This regime is the ambient channel for caller-supplied behavioral overrides to BUK primitives.
Unlike the file-sourced regimes,
{bure_regime}
variables are set directly in the caller's environment rather than read from an assignment file,
and it is kindled and validated when buc_command.sh is sourced.
Its inlet is
{bus_inlet_ambient}
— the one family whose values legitimately ride the session environment
and its inheritance
({BUr_q2m}).
Two sub-natures share the family, separated by doctrine:
operator comfort dials
({bure_countdown}, {bure_verbose}, {bure_color})
and the test tweak seam
({bure_tweak_name}, {bure_tweak_value}),
bounded below to sparing, one-at-a-time use.
