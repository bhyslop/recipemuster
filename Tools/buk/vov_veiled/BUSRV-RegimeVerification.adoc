{burv_regime}

This regime is harness-composed placement redirection:
it relocates the station-directory roots (output, temp, logs) for one launched child,
so a driving harness can observe, isolate, and chain the child's products.
The child's work is untouched —
only where its products land moves.
Read exclusively by the dispatch chain,
which folds each BURV value over its BURC/BURS counterpart at launch;
business code never reads BURV.

Three composers stand.
The BUT bridge (`zbuto_invoke` under `BUTE_BURV_ROOT`) and the theurge child launch each compose for one tabtarget invocation.
The JJ dispatch stirrup composes for one billet session —
there the session is the child, and its whole tabtarget population shares the redirect,
which is what makes a billet's tabtargets first-class: they log and chain exactly as in a hippodrome, into billet-scoped roots
(the whoever-dispatches-owns-the-knob protocol; JJSVD).
Innermost composition wins:
a composer nearer the child overrides an outer one.
