== Act II — The mounted work

== Act III — Wrap and teardown

//axhci_interaction
`jjcbs_session` `jjcbs_engine`
{jjdo_close} —
the wrap.
Its entry gate reads the billet against the sire's trunk first: a billet trailing trunk refuses here, an interdictum naming {jjdd_refit}, and nothing downstream runs.
Past the gate, remaining work is committed, the pace state mutates, and the same {jjdb_journal} bracket carries it into the studbook (the previous two interactions' shape, replayed).

//axhci_interaction
`jjcbs_engine` `jjcbs_hippodrome`
The converge: behind the work commit, the wrap bequeaths the billet's whole tree to the sire's trunk as one commit parented on trunk's tip — no checkout anywhere — so delivery is the wrap's own act and no hand merge follows it.
The {jjdb_journal} entry records the position produced as a second {jjdb_counterfoil} — the `Converge:` trailer, since a bequest leaves no ancestry a check could follow (wrap sheaf).

//axhci_interaction
`jjcbs_session` `jjcbs_operator`
The session ends and the operator is back at the shell.

//axhci_interaction
`jjcbs_dispatch` `jjcbs_hippodrome`
The {jjdd_stile_p} trailing step runs the exit litmus — pure git, no studbook read — and the billet passes: the wrapped work is committed and consigned, so the worktree holds nothing the branch and journal do not, and the stile destroys it on the spot, one line naming where the work stands.
The teardown actor is the stile — parent of the session it launched, standing outside the billet — never the session, which cannot remove its own floor; a billet that fails the litmus stands, one line naming the failed conjunct, and {jjdd_muck} is the operator's remedy.
