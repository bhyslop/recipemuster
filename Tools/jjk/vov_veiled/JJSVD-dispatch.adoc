This is a normative operation sheaf of the {jjs0_imprimatur}: binding — code, tests, and decisions may rely on it.

= Dispatch and Billet

The operation family at JJ's shell edge: two doors — {jjdd_saddle} and {jjdd_lunge} —
sharing one approach, the {jjdd_billet} a dispatched session runs in, the {jjdd_stile} that
brackets the whole crossing and clears its own billet at exit, the {jjdd_muck} door for what
a stile leaves standing, the {jjdd_refit} staleness remedy, and the {jjdd_cashier}.
Everything here composes {jjdf_farrier} primitives and the {jjdb_journal};
this sheaf owns no primitive of its own.

*The doors are the sole entrance*: every JJ-dispatched session arrives
through {jjdd_saddle} or {jjdd_lunge}, and the two share the one approach.
A bare hand-launched session carries no MCP registration and no JJ context — it is simply a
non-JJ session, forever legitimate as such;
no fallback paths until genuinely forced.
The consequence is load-bearing, not cosmetic: the entrance must be pleasant very early —
which is why the approach leads with no housekeeping at all: billet clearing rides the
{jjdd_stile_p} exit, never its entrance.

== The billet

[[jjdd_billet]]
//axo_entity
{jjdd_billet}::
The standing thing a dispatched session runs in: a root with a lifecycle on the partition
axis (farrier sheaf, Two axes — worktree for plain git, full clone the fallback for kinds
without worktrees), plus a branch-or-detached-tip and an env posture (the per-billet BURV
exports below).
The object noun completing the verbs-act-on-nouns grammar: mount acts on a pace,
{jjdd_saddle} creates a {jjdd_billet}, the {jjdd_stile} clears it at exit, and {jjdd_muck}
destroys the residue.
Boarded on arrival, kept for the duration, cleared at exit — never by the session inside it.
+
Two kinds, split by door.
A *pace billet* ({jjdd_saddle}) seats the pace's branch, which wears the {jjdd_livery} —
`jjls_pace/{catchword}_{coronet}` — and its dirname is `jjqb_{catchword}_{coronet}` per the
{jjdw_yard} charter: the one birth {jjdb_catchword} labels both, ref tail and dirname alike.
A *groom billet* ({jjdd_lunge}) seats a detached checkout at trunk tip — nothing must
survive it — and its dirname is `jjqb_{catchword}_{firemark}`, the same signet.
A {jjdd_lunge} aimed at a pace is no exception: its billet wears that pace's HEAT
{jjdt_firemark}, never the {jjdt_coronet} it grooms.
The leading serial is the {jjdb_catchword} the birth's dispatch record allocated (blotter
sheaf): it sorts the yard by birth and is what keeps concurrent groom billets of one heat
distinct.
The identity behind it types by length exactly as identities are everywhere;
a reader steps over the serial rather than parsing it, so the dirname stays a denormalized
label and never an identity.
+
*The dirname's identity is the yard's kind channel*, and that is what the firemark rule
above protects.
Two readers derive a billet's KIND from that identity's length alone, neither of them
asking git: the yard gate's YARD key, which scans for a {jjdt_coronet}-labelled entry and
would read a pace-aimed groom as the standing pace billet it is not (Yard step below);
and {jjdd_muck}'s resolution, which types a {jjdt_coronet} dirname as a pace billet and then
demands the seated {jjdd_livery} branch a detached groom can never show — stranding it as
unmuckable by machine.
A groom wearing a {jjdt_coronet} would corrupt both at once, and no kind-awareness taught to
one reader would reach the other.
So the aim rides the surfaces that carry meaning — the opening prompt and the dispatch
record — while the dirname carries only the label its two readers can safely type.
+
The two brands answer to different owners and so do not match:
the {jjdw_yard} signet marks JJ's own infield, where a bare body is unambiguous;
the {jjdd_livery} marks JJ's refs inside a repository JJ does not own.
Billets are {jjdw_infield} peers of their {jjdw_hippodrome} — peer placement is load-bearing
(station geography, cosmology sheaf) — and worktrees share the hippodrome's object store:
no history duplication, no fetch between billet and hippodrome;
the billet-side cost is working files and cold build artifacts only.
+
*Billets are session-transient; branches are durable.*
The pace branch is the WIP carrier across chats, journaled in pace state;
the worktree is disposable scaffolding, destroyed by its own {jjdd_stile_p} trailing step
whenever it passes the exit litmus and rematerialized by a later dispatch's ensure.
The weight of retaining work is borne by the branches and the {jjdb_journal}, never the
yard.
`git worktree list` is the authoritative local registry of which branch is seated where —
no worktree path is ever written into the {jjdb_studbook} (the no-worktree-paths rivet,
studbook sheaf).
+
[[jjdd_livery]]
//axk_premise
{jjdd_livery}::
The badge every JJ-authored branch name wears in a sire's ref store.
A bare identity body is a legitimate ref name, and that is the hazard:
JJ works in repositories owned by other organizations, where an unbadged five-character
branch claims globally invasive namespace and says nothing about whose it is or what it
carries.
The badge is the remedy — and the premise it bounds is that JJ *never* legislates a foreign
ref store, only conducts itself legibly within one.
+
The static sprue `jjls_` is the badge — `l` livery, `s` studbook, written honestly:
these branches are the work-repo objects the {jjdb_studbook_p} {jjdb_counterfoil_s} name.
The full literal token is the point: one grep over `jjls_` unites this definition, the code
constant, and every live branch in every sire.
A kind is a *roster word*, never a namespace letter, so a future kind costs a word and
nothing else:
+
* `jjls_pace/{catchword}_{coronet}` — LIVE. The pace's WIP carrier; the tail leads with the
  birth {jjdb_catchword} serial ahead of the coronet body (the carriage law below).
  Named for the pace, not the mount: the branch outlives every mount of it.
* `jjls_groom` — RESERVED, never populated.
  A groom billet is detached and carries nothing durable, so a branch under this word is a
  contract violation, not a line of work.
  The word is rostered — and parsed — precisely so that violation is nameable when met.
+
The sprue is the namespace root and the slash separates badge from identity, so kind lives
in the greppable token rather than a cryptic path segment;
git's ref store gives the whole `jjls_` family terminal exclusivity for free.
A {jjdb_pedigree} may record a path prefix nesting the whole badge, but only where a sire's
owner demands one — never by default.
The disengagement sweep is therefore `refs/heads/**/jjls_*`.
+
The body rides glyphless — a git ref is a foreign-traversed surface, so the sigil stays
behind ({jjdt_insignia} carriage law) — but never bare, on two counts.
A loose ref is a filename, and on a case-insensitive volume two bodies differing only in
letter case fuse onto one ref file; and a body derived from the coronet alone is the same ref
at every birth, so re-dispatching a pace would silently reuse one branch across occupancies.
The tail therefore leads with the birth {jjdb_catchword} — the same serial the dirname wears
({jjdw_yard}), one allocation worn as two denormalized labels, never a second journal write —
then `_`, then the bare body: `jjls_pace/200393_CAAB_`.
A decimal serial is stable under case-folding, so the composed name is case-fold-injective by
construction (case-distinct coronets keep distinct refs on any filesystem); and it is unique
per birth, so each occupancy takes its own ref and silent reuse under one name is structurally
impossible.
Reading the badge is how a branch name resolves back to an identity — strip any prefix by
finding the sprue-bearing segment, admit the roster word exactly, then type the tail by shape:
a serialed tail is a digit-run LONGER than any body, then `_`, then the body — the serial
stepped over and never interpreted, exactly as the dirname read steps over its own, so the
carriage law extends to the ref.
A bare-length tail also reads — a pre-serial billet branch, legible while one still stands —
and an interim *case-armored* tail (a case marker ahead of each character, twice the bare body
length) reads too: a closed, fading cohort of branches born before the serial, kept legible
until they age out.
Consumers *read* rather than compose wherever a pedigree is not already in hand, so a
recorded prefix can never defeat a comparison — and a per-birth serial is unguessable ahead of
the mint, so a consumer that must find a pace's branch enumerates and reads, never derives.

*Every dispatched session gets its own billet; the trunk ref advances only through the wrap ceremony's converge, under the operator's wrap confirmation.*
The {jjdw_hippodrome} is a lazy follower — its working tree pulls the sire's trunk at the operator's own cadence, and correctness never waits on that pull — while the trunk itself moves only when a wrap bequeaths a billet's estate onto it (wrap sheaf).
Session isolation is structural, not disciplinary — even a "read-only" groom can run builds.
Git itself enforces the split: a branch checked out in one worktree cannot be checked out in
another — pace billets own unique branches and groom billets are detached, so the constraint
never binds, and a billet mechanically cannot seat the trunk branch while the
{jjdw_hippodrome} holds it.

== Ground

[[jjdd_ground]]
{jjdd_ground}::
Where a session stands, reduced from {jjdf_identify}'s two facts — seat and line of work — and
nothing else.
Distinct from the {jjdf_foreign_ground} the {jjdf_identify} contract already names: that is a
tree no kind claims at all, the fair-faced decline at the {jjdf_palisade};
a session that has reached a JJ verb stands on ground JJ does claim, and this taxonomy names
which of four it is.
+
--
* *Hippodrome* — the primary seat: the operator's own clone, a {jjdw_hippodrome}.
* *Pace billet* — a partition seat on a branch wearing the pace {jjdd_livery}
  (`jjls_pace/{coronet}`): the one ground a mounted pace runs on.
* *Groom billet* — a partition seat, detached at trunk tip: a {jjdd_lunge}'s assessment ground,
  carrying nothing durable.
* *Unboarded* — a partition seat on a branch wearing no pace livery: an operator's own
  {jjdw_infield} worktree, or the reserved `jjls_groom` word a groom billet must never populate
  — a ground nameable precisely because the badge parses (The billet, above).
--
+
The ground is never read from a dirname — a dirname is a label a reader steps over ({jjdw_yard}
charter) — but from the seat and line of work {jjdf_identify} resolves; the ground is their
reduction, and no verb re-derives it.
+
*The ground guards.*
Every verb declares a ground requirement, and the {jjdd_stile_p} dispatch door resolves the
ground once and judges it once — ahead of every handler, so no command carries a ground rule of
its own.
Two buckets:
+
--
* *Own pace billet* — the mount ({jjdo_orient}), the notch ({jjdo_record}), and the wrap
  ({jjdo_close}).
  Each acts on a billet's own tree — a mount takes up the pace's tree, a notch commits work-repo
  files, a wrap delivers the billet's estate to trunk — so off a pace billet each refuses
  fair-faced and names its remedy: *saddle the pace from the shell* for a mount or wrap, *slate a
  pace for this work, then saddle it* for a notch.
  The billet must be the aim's own: a mount re-orienting the pace it already seats is legal
  (re-orientation, not a remount), but a mount or wrap aimed at another pace refuses.
  The notch alone admits a second aim — its parent heat's {jjdt_firemark} beside its own
  {jjdt_coronet}, so heat-affiliated work commits from the pace's billet; the empty notch is
  own-pace-billet like every other.
* *Free* — every other verb.
  The studbook reads and writes alike ({jjdo_show}, {jjdo_paddock}, {jjdo_log}, {jjdo_brief},
  {jjdo_coronets}, {jjdo_enroll}, {jjdo_redocket}, {jjdo_curry}, and the rest) run from any
  ground: grooming any heat from any billet is deliberately legal, because cross-heat reference
  is how detail work happens, and these verbs touch the {jjdb_studbook}, never the work repo.
--
+
*The groom posture.*
A groom billet is detached and ephemeral: work-repo edits have no durable home there, and the
notch refuses them.
So {jjdo_open} and every groom-ground read lead with that posture in one line, and the
{jjdd_lunge}'s opening prompt carries the same sentence — discovery that warrants work becomes a
slated pace, never a commit here.
A groom billet that nonetheless holds a work-repo commit is a pace in denial, caught without a
guard of its own: the notch ground gate refuses the commit at the moment of temptation, and any
content-bearing commit that reaches the tree by another route fails the {jjdd_stile_p} exit
litmus and stands for {jjdd_muck} (a content-less marker clears, so the everyday {jjdo_open}
echo is no denial).
The remedy is always the same — slate the work, then saddle it.
+
*The spent-pace posture.*
A pace billet's own tree can outlive the pace it was cut for: the wrap that runs from that
billet can leave its pace {jjdpe_resolved} (complete or abandoned) while the billet itself
stands, and re-saddle deliberately opens a fresh epoch from trunk rather than reoccupying the
spent one — so nothing ever converges a commit landed there afterward, and it strands silently
on a branch no ceremony returns to.
The seated coronet alone cannot see this — the branch still wears the pace's badge whether the
pace is open or resolved — so the notch alone (never the mount, never the wrap) also reads the
seated pace's own state.
Once it is {jjdpe_resolved}, the notch refuses fair-faced at the moment of temptation, keyed on
ground and state together and never on the commit's own affiliation — a pace-affiliated notch, a
heat-affiliated one, and the empty notch all refuse alike — naming its remedy: exit the session,
since new work is a fresh slate-and-saddle.
An open (rough or bridled) pace's own billet notches exactly as the bucket above describes,
unchanged.

== The doors

JJ opens the Claude Code sessions we work in.
The operator knows the verb at chat-top, and the session's {jjdd_billet} must be right at
launch — mid-session directory change neither reloads project context nor survives the cd
prohibition.
The doors are operator-typed at the shell, from within the elected clone: *cwd elects the
clone* — the pace cannot, since parallel clones of one sire are all legitimate
{jjdw_hippodrome_s} — and either door works from trunk or billet alike
({jjdf_identify} resolves either).

[[jjdd_saddle]]
{jjdd_saddle}::
The work dispatch.
A {jjdt_coronet} saddles that pace;
a {jjdt_firemark} saddles the heat's *next actionable pace* — mounting a heat has always
meant mounting its first pace, and the firemark spelling keeps that meaning.
Either way the session gets a pace billet, and the launch consumes the pace's designation
per the two-source choice (Session launch below).
The bash-layer verb is deliberately not "mount" — Unix owns that word; the two-layer split
is the codex's vocabulary-isolation doctrine at a new boundary.
+
This definition is the word's one dispatch-layer home.

[[jjdd_lunge]]
{jjdd_lunge}::
The groom dispatch: working the horse from the ground on the long line, deliberately
unmounted — assessment without riding.
A {jjdt_firemark} grooms that heat; a {jjdt_coronet} grooms that pace.
The target types by length exactly as identities do everywhere, and the two aims share one
ground: either way the session gets a groom billet (detached at trunk tip) and the launch
takes the judgment constant (Session launch below).
The aim differs, the posture does not — which is why the pace groom is this door widened and
not a door of its own.
+
The aim reaches the session through the *opening prompt*, which names a pace in the
qualified emission form (`₢{firemark}·{coronet}`) so it arrives already in its heat's
context, and through the *dispatch record*, whose sigil follows the target rather than the
door.
The billet's dirname does not carry it: both aims wear the heat {jjdt_firemark} there, for
the kind-channel reason homed at {jjdd_billet} above.
+
A {jjdt_coronet} naming no pace refuses — the lookup is owed anyway, since the heat the
dirname wears is read from it.
A pace's STATE is not read: a groom assesses a docket, and a settled pace's docket is as
legible as a live one's.

[[jjdd_cashier]]
//axd_ashlar
{jjdd_cashier}::
The door that dismisses a derelict lock-holder from service:
`sight` every JJ {jjdb_blotter} lock, report the observed {jjdb_guidon_s}, then lease-guarded
`pluck` per lock, never blind.
Outside the {jjdd_stile_p} approach — no dispatch crosses it, and nothing composes it.
The door cites the {jjdb_break} per lock and adds nothing to its mechanism (journal sheaf):
what the door contributes is *deliberateness*, which is the door's property and never the
sequence's.
Precedent `/vvc-BREAK-LOCK`.
+
*Two modes.*
*Sight-and-report* is read-only and always safe — it takes nothing, breaks nothing, and is
the mode an agent or a script may run freely.
*Break* mutates, and passes the confirm gate below.
+
*The roster.* The door sights a roster of {jjdb_blotter} configs, engine-known like every
{jjdb_blotter_p} own coordinates (entity sheaf, bootstrap config) — never pedigree-resolved.
Today the roster holds one entry, the {jjdb_studbook};
the mews fleet store joins it when its contract lands, and not before — its coordinates
invented early would be a forward reference with nothing behind it.
The `vvx` commit lock (`refs/vvg/locks/vvx`, precedent above) is a *different apparatus* with
its own door and is NOT in this roster:
it guards a consumer repo's commits, not a {jjdb_blotter}, and it speaks no {jjdb_guidon}.
Stated so that a later hand does not unify two locks that share only a shape.
+
*The confirm gate*, and what it must show.
The gate is this door's contract, not the {jjdb_break_p} — a break fired without a human
answering is this door failing at the one thing it exists to do.
Per lock, the gate shows:
+
--
* the four {jjdb_guidon} fields, with acquire time rendered as an *age* — the operator judges
  staleness on age, never on a timestamp they must subtract in their head;
* a *liveness warning* that warns and never blocks.
  A ceremony runs in seconds, so a lock under a minute old is probably a live writer, not a
  corpse.
  The warning is advice to a human who may know something the clock does not (that the station
  is unplugged, that the process is gone);
  a door that refused would simply be un-runnable in the case it exists for.
* the *differentiated consequence*, per `JJr_c4e` (journal sheaf):
  breaking a live *writer* costs that writer its ceremony and nothing more — the lease
  refuses its `consign` and *nothing lands*;
  breaking a live *acting reader* is the accepted exposure — it may act on a stale image, and
  nothing catches it.
  The two are not the same risk, and a gate that collapsed them would teach the operator the
  wrong thing about their own decision;
* *which store* the lock guards — a roster of one today, and a wrong-store break tomorrow is
  exactly the mistake a one-line report invites.
--
+
*Discoverability.* A stranded lock surfaces to the operator as a `LockHeld` refusal at the
next locked {jjdb_studbook} write — the yard step's birth record on the entrance path, or
any journal ceremony.
That refusal text must NAME this door.
A cure the sufferer cannot find is not a cure, and the refusal is the only moment the
operator is guaranteed to be looking.

== The stile

[[jjdd_stile]]
{jjdd_stile}::
The application lifecycle as one bounded crossing: a door, the approach below, the launched
session, and the trailing step below — one process, entered at the shell and left at
session exit.
The word is literal: a stile is a structure for crossing a fence, and the crossing is the
thing — the {jjdd_billet} is ground the stile stands up on the way in and typically clears
on the way out.
The door driver spawns the session and waits, so the stile is still standing — outside the
billet, parent of the session it launched — when the session returns;
that geometry is what the trailing step rides, and it is why no occupancy record exists
anywhere: the destructor is the occupant's own parent, acting only after its child has
exited.
The one attended gate INSIDE the crossing is the resume confirm (Yard step above): the door
driver, still the future session's parent, holds for the operator's word before re-entering a
standing billet — the occupancy oracle that keeps "no record exists anywhere" true even as a
billet is reused rather than born.
A resumed billet is no new kind and mints no serial; the trailing step's exit litmus reads it
exactly as any other pace billet.
A future immediate-work crossing (pace-free, journal-born) is a stile kind, not a new noun;
kinds cost a roster word, exactly as {jjdd_billet} kinds do.
+
*The approach.*
The crossing's opening segment — the one shared sequence behind both doors, where a door
contributes only its target typing and its tier source, never its own approach:
+
. *{jjdf_identify}* at the captured invocation path — the door captures cwd exactly once;
  a decline is the fair-faced {jjdf_foreign_ground} rejection.
. *Pedigree lookup* — a {jjdb_studbook} read, no lock, one indirection:
  derived key → sire → {jjdb_pedigree};
  a key resolving no sire is the unrecorded-sire rejection, and a claiming
  kind that contradicts the recorded kind is the record/ground-drift rejection
  ({jjdf_identify} contract, farrier sheaf).
. *Yard step* — the yard gate, then, on a clear pace, the birth record.
  At most one live pace billet per {jjdt_coronet}, and the gate is what forks on it: a saddle
  whose pace ALREADY has a billet neither silently rejoins a live worktree nor mints a rival
  past a missed seat — it RESUMES a cleanly-standing one under an attended confirm, and REFUSES
  an anomalous one, HERE, before the birth record's journal write and before any session spawn.
  The gate reads two keys, because a standing billet can evade either alone.
  The {jjdd_livery}-branch SEAT the kind's own partition registry records (the authority for a
  billet on its own branch) — enumerated and matched by the {jjdt_coronet} behind each badge,
  since a per-birth serial makes the branch name unguessable ahead of the mint — is a CLEANLY
  standing billet, so it resumes: the standing worktree and its seated serial branch are reused
  in place, no fresh birth and no fresh serial, launched with the mount prompt once the confirm
  answers.
  The {jjdt_coronet}-labelled YARD entry (which catches a partition the seat-read misses —
  a detached tip, a lost registration, a pre-livery bare-branch billet — its dirname wearing the
  coronet whatever its HEAD points at) has its livery branch seated nowhere, so re-entering it
  would need branch-resolution this arm does not own: it refuses, naming the partition and
  {jjdd_muck} as the remedy.
  Neither key is the yard-by-name mistake it might look like: a dirname is a label and a serialed
  branch is unguessable, so the seat key enumerates the registry and reads the coronet behind each
  badge, and the yard key is only the second, independent read the seat-read alone would miss.
  The resume confirm is the occupancy oracle the geometry keeps in place of any record (The stile
  below): it NAMES the live-session possibility — the standing partition, its seated branch, its
  dirty paths by name — and the attending operator answers occupied-vs-vacated.
  Checkout exclusivity is NOT this enforcement: it guards only the narrow seat arm — a second
  worktree add on an already-seated branch — never the dispatch, so without the gate a re-saddle
  rejoins a live worktree (a second session into one billet) or, where the seat-read misses, mints
  a rival past it.
  A groom billet seats no branch and passes the gate untouched — concurrent grooms of one heat
  are deliberately legal, told apart by their serials alone.
  It passes the YARD key by construction rather than by exemption: a groom dirname wears a
  {jjdt_firemark} whatever the {jjdd_lunge} was aimed at ({jjdd_billet} above), so a
  {jjdt_coronet} lookup can never match one.
  Only when both keys are silent is the pace clear to be born: the birth is a {jjdb_studbook} WRITE
  (the dispatch record, blotter sheaf), online and refusable under a held {jjdb_guidon}, the accepted
  cost of a durable birth timeline, and the fresh billet's DIRNAME wears the serial it allocates.
  The BRANCH it seats, though, is not always fresh: it is the pace's CURRENT LIVE LINE, resolved
  through the journal (Current-line resolution, studbook sheaf) — the coronet's founding birth since
  its last wrap — never composed anew when a prior line already stands, and never decided by ref
  enumeration, which is race-partial.
  The read takes the journal as it stood BEFORE this dispatch's own birth, so the question stays
  "was there a prior line"; a coronet with none founds a fresh one, its dirname and branch sharing
  the single serial.
  This is what reawakens the ensure's seat and adopt arms below: they probe a name resolved from the
  record — one that actually stands — rather than a fresh name that by construction stands nowhere.
. *Billet ensure* — seat-or-adopt-or-create ({jjdf_billet} facet);
  a groom billet in reuse re-detaches to trunk tip.
  A branch the registry seats elsewhere refuses by name here — seat-vestige or line-seated,
  each carrying its own remedy (farrier sheaf) — so the yard step never invents an anomaly
  taxonomy of its own.
  The three arms are read in one order, and the order is the whole of adopt-never-fork:
  a line standing at home re-seats (`billet_seat`);
  a line absent at home but standing abroad is another station's pushed work and is adopted
  (`billet_adopt`), so one pace is one line of work across stations;
  only a line standing nowhere is born (`billet_create`).
  The abroad question is the one answer a fetch changes, so the ensure gleans just before
  asking it and never earlier: the counterpart read is network-silent, and a stale no forks.
. *`glean`* — the approach fetches and never merges;
  staleness becomes known here so the open can report it, and {jjdd_refit} is the remedy.
. *BURV export* — the per-billet env posture (BUK meld below).
. *Provision* — JJ context and MCP registration, session-scoped (Launch-time provisioning
  below).
. *Launch* — start Claude Code inside the {jjdd_billet} with the engagement verb as opening
  prompt, at the (tier, effort) the two-source choice yields (Session launch below).
  The interior launch primitive is stirrup — hearting, pace-blind, parameterized (billet,
  tier, opening prompt); pace-coupling lives in the caller, and later dispatch surfaces
  reuse the same approach and primitive, never a parallel launcher.
+
*Report precedes launch.*
The door's report introduces the session it launches, so it reaches the operator's console
BEFORE the Launch step hands over the terminal — the whole resolved report (billet, launch
tier and effort, opening prompt, and any staleness notice), never trailing the session's exit.
The console-handoff is the approach's last act for exactly this reason: a report emitted after it
would land beneath the very session it was meant to precede.
On a resume the standing-billet report and the attended confirm ride the same precedence: both
land before the handoff.
+
Mount absorbs cold start — billet ensure, glean, orient are the approach's work, and no
separate cold-start engagement verb exists.
+
*The trailing step.*
The stile's closing act, after the session returns and ahead of its own exit: run the exit
litmus against the billet, and destroy a passing one (`billet_remove`, un-forced — the
op's refuse-on-dirt is the same verdict `comb` renders, since ignored files are invisible
to both and cold build residue therefore never blocks).
Automatic, no confirm — the litmus is the proof that destruction loses nothing, and that
proof is what entitles the automation.
One line reports the outcome either way: a cleared billet names where the work stands;
a standing billet names the failed conjunct and {jjdd_muck} as the remedy.
The session's own exit code is the stile's — trailing-step trouble reports and never masks
it.
The per-billet scratch dies with a cleared billet, cleared in the same act as the worktree;
a standing billet's scratch stands too — logs are forensics only for work that stands, and
{jjdd_muck} clears them with the billet's residue.
+
*The exit litmus*, kind-aware, refuse-and-stand:
+
--
* A *pace billet* passes when `comb` is clean AND either of two passes clears it. The
  *custody pass* — `sync_state` shows its tip not ahead of its own counterpart, every commit
  already in remote custody — clears a consigned-not-wrapped billet whose work is safe on its
  pushed branch. The *content-proof pass* — its content beyond trunk's custody base is
  `reachable` (farrier sheaf) — clears a dropped no-work billet whose only commit is the
  {jjdo_open} marker, though it was never consigned, because a derivable custody base is proof,
  not ignorance. The two passes are additive; only unconsigned content, or total ignorance,
  stands.
* A *groom billet* passes when `comb` is clean AND its content beyond trunk's custody base is
  `reachable` (farrier sheaf) — every commit the tip added is tree-identical to its parent, so
  a marker-only commit (every dispatch's {jjdo_open} echo) carries no content and passes, while
  a raw detached commit with real content stands. A groom is never consigned, so the
  content-proof pass is its whole litmus.
* Refusal remedies nothing: the trailing step never lodges, never consigns, never
  publishes an off-ceremony commit — the billet stands for {jjdd_muck} or a later clean
  exit.
* Total ignorance stands: no counterpart locally known — nor any merge-base with one — means
  nothing can be proven held, and a litmus that cannot prove must not destroy. A derivable
  custody base is NOT ignorance: content proven empty against it clears. The opposite polarity
  from `outstripped`, whose warning must not cry on ignorance.
--

== Muck

[[jjdd_muck]]
//axd_ashlar
{jjdd_muck}::
Mucking out the stable by hand: the operator-directed destroy door for a named
{jjdd_billet} the {jjdd_stile_p} trailing step left standing — dirty, stranded work
aboard, or orphaned by a killed door.
Operator-typed like the {jjdd_cashier}, outside the {jjdd_stile_p} approach: no dispatch crosses it,
and nothing composes it.
It is the constellation's one deliberate data-loss surface;
everything else refuses loss by construction, and that division of labor is the design —
the trailing step automates only what provably loses nothing, and every lossy call is
attended here.
No retention window, no liveness join: the operator names the billet — a {jjdw_yard}
dirname, or the identity behind one — and the plan's report is the occupancy evidence a
human weighs.
+
*Plan-then-confirm, never silent.*
The plan resolves the named billet and shows the operator what the decision needs:
+
--
* the billet's kind and seat, and the *dirty paths by name* — what destroy would lose is
  never summarized as a count;
* the branch posture (`sync_state`): commits not yet in remote custody are named as loss,
  never as advice;
* the pace's own state — resolved or still open — read through the journal sheaf's read
  bracket (an acting read: the snapshot arms this confirm), with the wrap
  {jjdb_counterfoil_s} as evidence, never as gate: the operator may destroy a mid-work
  billet deliberately.
--
+
*The destroy.*
One door, one act: the confirmed destroy loses the named dirty paths.
The removal is `billet_remove` behind its explicit force — the confirmed destroy is
the force's only caller.
+
*Scratch.*
Scratch dies with its billet: the door clears the destroyed billet's own scratch sibling
with it, and nothing else — {jjdd_muck} is a billet singleton, so its sweep never reaches
past the billet named in its own confirmed plan.
Scratch orphaned by any other route (a killed door, a crash) is not this door's target and
is left for hand cleaning.

== Refit

[[jjdd_refit]]
{jjdd_refit}::
The session-facing staleness remedy: when the ground moves, refit.
Staleness is drift, never corruption — the billet branch is additive and the {jjdb_studbook}
advances under its own lock — so no ordinary verb refuses on it: {jjdo_open} leads its report
with the staleness warning and names {jjdd_refit} as the remedy, and every other command
proceeds without a staleness word of its own.
The lone exception is the wrap ceremony, whose entry gate refuses a wrap on a billet trailing
the sire's trunk — an interdictum naming refit (wrap sheaf), because bequeathing an estate
onto a trunk the billet has never enfolded would lose that trunk work.
There refit is not a recommendation but the blocking remedy: the operator runs it, and the
wrap re-attempts on a billet now holding trunk's tip and passes by construction.
+
Behavior: `enfold` trunk into the billet branch — merge, never rebase: journaled
{jjdb_counterfoil_s} survive, and the branch's interior history never reaches trunk anyway,
since the wrap's converge squashes the estate onto trunk's tip (wrap sheaf) — then `consign`
the merge immediately: the operation exists to clear staleness for every station and runs
online by construction;
offline degrades to warn-and-proceed.
On conflict the attended session resolves as its first order of business — *attended* is the
load-bearing property, not tier;
{jjdd_saddle} may bump the tier when it detects a pending conflict, but tier-escalation
machinery is deferred past the MVP (breeze-era).

== Session launch

The tier vocabulary is the vendor's own family words — a Palisade admission at one membrane,
never a JJ mint (the {jjdf_palisade} posture applied to model families).
No tier categories are hardlined: a tier is just a name, and category judgment lives at each
policy site as an explicit name-set, never as an attribute a family IS.
Effort anchors transparently on the vendor's effort classification, likewise unminted;
effort never rides the MCP wire — it is designation-and-dispatch data, and the orient guard
reads tier alone.

One roster table, baked into the engine as a constant (the {jjdb_blotter_p} engine-known
posture): family name → launch model ID + valid effort set, no default columns.
Every kind of vendor drift lands as an edit to this one table, and a surprise is captured as
a spook.
Stirrup is the table's one launch consumer — callers speak tier words, never model IDs, and
an invalid (family, effort) pair refuses fair-facedly.
The fable family stays a roster row no launch policy names until its pricing settles;
opus is the working judgment tier.

The (tier, effort) choice has exactly two sources, split by door:

* {jjdd_lunge} launches the *judgment constant* — opus/xhigh, one named cell in the engine —
  for either aim.
  A pace-aimed {jjdd_lunge} deliberately does not read that pace's designation: a designation
  governs who may EXECUTE a docket, and a groom assesses one rather than working it, so
  grooming a pace bridled at a sub-frontier tier is judgment work like every other groom.
* {jjdd_saddle} launches the pace's designation exactly;
  on an unbridled pace it launches the same judgment constant — undesignated work is
  judgment work: such a session plans toward a bridle or simply finishes fast.
  A designation without effort launches with the effort knob omitted — the vendor default
  governs; JJ invents nothing.

Guard permanence: every command registers its guard bucket explicitly, no default — absence
fails loud.
The docket-authoring gate is an explicit accept-set of family names beside the roster;
an unknown family sits in no accept-set by construction — OPEN reads only, until a
deliberate roster-and-set edit.

Programmatic session dispatch for personal tooling is documented product surface (headless
mode, Agent SDK, `--model`);
recording that standing determination here is what keeps it from being re-litigated from
memory.

The permission posture is a second vendor surface stirrup speaks to, admitted at the same
membrane as the tier-and-effort Palisade above: `--permission-mode auto`, composed
unconditionally for every launched session, both doors alike. Auto mode is the vendor's
classifier-gated autonomy (status line: `auto mode on`): a separate classifier model reviews
each act before it runs — in-billet reads and edits pass without review, and everything else,
an out-of-billet gazette write included, routes to the classifier rather than to an operator
prompt. The classifier's default blocks align with the JJ conduct core's own git
prohibitions — the discard family (`reset --hard`, `restore`, `checkout`-to-discard,
`clean`, `stash drop`) — so the gate outside the billet rhymes with the conduct the session
already carries. The launch flag is the whole channel: the vendor ignores a repo-settings
grant of auto mode by design (a repository cannot grant itself autonomy), so the
saddle-written billet settings neither can nor need to speak it. Known boundary, accepted:
haiku-family models do not support auto mode on any vendor provider, and the flag's fallback
on an unsupported model is undocumented — observed at the next sub-frontier dispatch, not
engineered around.

== The BUK meld

BUK dispatch self-anchors — cwd normalizes to the invoked tree's root, and moorings ride
committed in any worktree — so BUK cannot tell a {jjdd_billet} from a clone, and the station
family already resolves to the {jjdw_infield} (peer placement load-bearing, cosmology
sheaf).
The genuine hazard is concurrency, not geometry: shared per-dispatch output state
(`../output-buk/current/`, `logs-buk/last.txt`).
The {jjdd_stile_p} approach closes it by exporting per-billet `BURV_OUTPUT_ROOT_DIR`,
`BURV_TEMP_ROOT_DIR`, and `BURV_LOG_DIR` — absolute paths, the whoever-dispatches-owns-the-knob
protocol; theurge needs no change (it sets BURV innermost, and innermost wins).
Governing sentence: the launched session sees exactly what a hand-launched
hippodrome session would have seen — operator ambient (`BURE_*` and vendor env)
intact — plus the per-billet BURV trio; everything the door's own dispatch chain
exported stops at the stirrup. The stirrup composes the BURV trio and strips every
such export at the same boundary (BUr_q2m: inheritance is never a regime inlet) —
the whole `BURD_*`/`BURC_*` family (dispatch-mode flags, parsed-tabtarget state,
regime-config re-derivations) plus the trampoline's ungoverned `JJSL_INVOKE_DIR`
cwd capture, by family membership rather than a hand-picked subset — because the
launched session is a first-class dispatch context whose tabtargets self-log into
the billet-scoped roots and re-derive their own dispatch state fresh, exactly as
in a hippodrome.
Named behavior change: chain facts (`current/` → `previous/`) become *billet-scoped* — a
tabtarget inside the billet and one in a trunk terminal no longer see each other's flow,
which also closes a latent cross-clone chain race that predates billets.

== Launch-time provisioning

{jjdw_hippodrome_s} carry no JJ inserts (cosmology sheaf), so MCP config, the verb table,

opening prompt — never from repo-resident inserts;
the project's own CLAUDE.md, whatever the vanilla project ships, still governs its code.

The destination is generated, work-scoped context: context honed
to the elected heat or pace — acronym rows the {jjdl_tackle} bindings name, the verb table
cut to the session type.
Default stance until that intelligence exists: retain everything the current inserts carry,
removing pieces only as honing is actually built.
Honing is prediction, and starvation fails silent — the full table is also the index of what

== Stirruped entry

A dispatched session arrives with the engagement verb in its opening prompt, so target
election shifts form: the door already elected the pace or heat, and the opening prompt
carries it — the hand-opened session's target-election dance is not performed.
A session saddled onto a pace bridled at a *sub-frontier* tier follows the designee protocol
(orient, work, record, landing — never wrap);
a judgment session — {jjdd_lunge}, {jjdd_saddle} on an unbridled pace, or {jjdd_saddle} on a
pace bridled at a *frontier* tier — keeps the full ceremony.
A frontier designation names which tier may act, never that the acting session is a designee:
demoting it would make the frontier tiers undesignable in practice, forcing judgment work to
park in an unbridled pace and stripping the designation of the one thing it records.
The kit-core protocol texts pick this seam up at implementation.
