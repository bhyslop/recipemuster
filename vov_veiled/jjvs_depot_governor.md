# <a id="rbsod_scenario"></a>Levy a Depot and Gird Its First Governor

## <a id="rbsvd_walk"></a>The walk

### Goal

A depot stands for this clone with its first governor: the manor's substrate stands, its APIs enabled, its billing linked, its workforce pool and terrier bucket founded; the federation trust is seated under that pool by the Affiance; the depot is levied under the cloud prefix this clone's depot settings name; its founding governor is seated by the Gird; and that governor holds a Sederunt opened by the Novate.

The documents to take up, in this order: the `README.md` at the root of the clone; the onboarding guide it tells you to run; from that guide's start menu, the payor track; and the federation guide the payor track names, where the track sends you back to it for the governor's subject.
Take up the payor track at the step "Instaurate the Manor" and walk it to its end, the Novate that opens the governor's first Sederunt, and stop there, where the track ends.
Other documents stand in the clone — the other tracks, the guides other commands print, and the project's code and configuration files beyond those a direction names — and are left unopened, as a newcomer might not think to open them.

The ground this walk stands on: an earlier walk on this same clone left a payor standing, its OAuth credential installed and its payor, federation and depot settings filled, as the payor track's steps before "Instaurate the Manor" leave them.
Every step before the Novate runs under that payor credential, so no sign-in is owed until the Novate.
The citizen the Gird seats is the attendant's own user at the Microsoft Entra tenant.
Where the track asks for a value of your own choosing, choose it yourself and say what you chose, save the workforce pool's ID, which the attendant names.

### The browser window

A browser window on a profile of its own, signed in to the Microsoft Entra admin center as the tenant's administrator, and to no other account.
You read the console in it yourself, save the acts below.

### Acts reserved to the attendant

Each act below is the attendant's to perform, never yours.
Before you hand one over, read what the directions say for it, look at the page or the command it names, and say whether the two agree; then tell the attendant exactly what to do, and wait until they say it is done.
Where an act is a command the track gives, the attendant runs it at their own terminal, from the clone's root, exactly as the track gives it, and reads back to you what it prints.

Reserved to the attendant at the terminal, committing money: the Instaurate door the track gives links the attendant's billing account to the payor project.
The same run founds the manor's workforce pool in the attendant's organization and its terrier bucket, which stand after the walk; they are reserved with it, by name.

Reserved to the attendant at the terminal, granting access — seating the federation's provider under the workforce pool: the Affiance door the track gives, naming the federation directory the federation guide wrote the trust values into.

Reserved to the attendant at the terminal, founding a project: the levy door the track gives creates the depot's own Google Cloud project, once you have committed whatever stands pending in the clone as the track directs.

Reserved to the attendant at the terminal, committing money: that same levy run links the depot's project to the attendant's billing account, so the attendant's running it is this act too, and nothing further is asked of them.

Reserved to the attendant at the terminal, granting access — seating the depot's first governor: the Gird door the track gives, naming as its subject the Object ID of the attendant's own user, which you read at the window where the federation guide says it is found.

Reserved to the attendant at the window, proving identity: when the Novate door you run prints a sign-in address and a code, the attendant opens that address, enters the code, and signs in as the user the Gird just seated.

Reserved to the attendant at the window, granting access — consenting at the citizen's first sign-in: where the tenant then asks the user to consent to the scopes the sign-in requests, the attendant accepts, as the federation guide says to expect.
