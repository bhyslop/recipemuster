# Prove a Crucible's Containment Under Attack

## The walk

### Goal

The tadmor Crucible has stood Charged and been attacked, as the onboarding's evaluator track teaches it: its Sentry and its Bottle images stand built on this machine by the Kludge, the Hallmark of each written into the tadmor Nameplate and committed; the track's adversarial suite has run in full against the Charged Crucible, and you have read what it reported; you have listed the suite's cases and run one of them alone, as the track offers; and the Crucible stands Quenched.

The documents to take up, in this order: the `README.md` at the root of the clone; the onboarding guide it tells you to run; and, from that guide's start menu, the evaluator track that proves containment under attack, among its kludged Crucibles.
Walk that track to its end, its closing section "What you evaluated", and stop there.
Other documents stand in the clone — the other tracks, the guides other commands print, and the project's code and configuration files beyond those a direction names — and are left unopened, as a newcomer might not think to open them.

The ground this walk stands on: the clone the environment walk configured, its station file validating, and nothing of the cloud.
This walk signs in to nothing and asks nothing of a payor or a depot, so it stands alike on a clone the cloud walks have also run on, and uses nothing they left.
The clone shipped with the Hallmark fields of every Nameplate blank, and those of the tadmor Nameplate stand blank when you begin.
Docker stands installed and running on this machine, and no tadmor Crucible stands Charged on it, from this clone or from any other.

This walk is walked alone: it needs no browser window, and reserves no act to a person.
